Figure 3: Lead Time Distribution
============================================================

What it shows:
Distribution of lead time (steps between alert and actual flood)
for normal_wet and extreme_wet scenarios.

How to read:
- Box: interquartile range (25th-75th percentile)
- Red line: median lead time
- Whiskers: min/max (excluding outliers)
- Higher values = more advance warning time

Interpretation:
MAS+ML provides comparable or better lead time than baseline,
with more consistent performance (tighter distribution).
