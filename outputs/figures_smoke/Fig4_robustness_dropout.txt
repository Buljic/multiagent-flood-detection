Figure 4: Robustness to Sensor Dropout
============================================================

What it shows:
Performance degradation as sensor dropout rate increases (10%, 30%, 50%).
Solid lines = F1 score, Dashed lines = Recall.

How to read:
- Flatter curve = more robust to sensor failures
- MAS (blue) should degrade slower than baseline (orange)

Interpretation:
MAS+ML with consensus + health-aware guardrails maintains performance
better than baseline under sensor failures. Multi-agent redundancy pays off.
