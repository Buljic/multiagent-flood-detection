Figure 1: Timeline - Extreme Wet
============================================================

What it shows:
- Panel 1: ML risk prediction over time with alert threshold
- Panel 2: Alert states from MAS (blue) vs Baseline (orange)
- Panel 3: Ground truth flood occurrence (red)

How to read:
- Look for alerts (Panel 2) appearing BEFORE floods (Panel 3) = good lead time
- Compare MAS vs Baseline alert patterns: fewer toggles = more stable

Interpretation:
MAS+ML provides smoother, more stable alerts with better timing compared to
baseline threshold heuristic. Guardrails prevent flapping while maintaining sensitivity.
